# Qingheng AI — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 29, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Qingheng AI is an independent nutrition and weight management application available through the Anna platform.

We are committed to minimizing unnecessary data collection and providing users with control over their personal records.

## 2. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, Qingheng AI processes:

- Food descriptions, quantities, and meal records.
- Estimated calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat.
- Weight measurements and measurement dates.
- Age, height, weight, sex-based calculation settings, activity level, and nutrition goals.
- Optional food photographs submitted for AI analysis.

## 3. AI-Powered Food and Nutrition Analysis

AI food analysis is optional.

When you explicitly agree and request an analysis, the selected food description and photograph, if provided, are transmitted through Anna's hosted AI services.

Anna and its AI service providers may process this information to generate nutritional estimates.

Qingheng AI does not independently retain the submitted photographs, raw AI responses, or unsaved meal descriptions in its food journal.

AI-generated estimates must be reviewed before being saved.


### Selected-day nutrition insights

Nutrition insights require a separate opt-in. When you request them, the selected day's recorded food names, serving amounts and known nutrients, your profile and calculated starting targets are sent through Anna's hosted AI service to its model provider. The request identifies missing nutrition data and incomplete-day limits. Switching the selected date resets this consent in the application.

Advice is displayed temporarily and is not saved in the food journal. The application does not claim that Anna or its model providers retain no requests; provider processing and retention are governed by their own policies. Nutrition estimates are not diagnoses or treatment.

## 4. Data Storage

Within Anna, confirmed food records, weight measurements, and nutrition settings are stored using Anna's user-scoped application storage.

The Anna Edition does not connect to the original Qingheng Cloudflare database.

In standalone local previews, records are stored in the user's browser.

Platform-level logging, security processing, and retention are governed by Anna's applicable policies.

## 5. Data Sharing

Qingheng AI does not independently sell personal information or use nutrition records for advertising.

Information necessary for the application's functions is processed through the Anna platform. AI submissions may be processed by Anna's third-party model providers.

For details, please review the Anna Privacy Policy:

https://anna.partners/legal/privacy

## 6. Your Data Controls

The application provides options to:

- Review and correct AI-generated nutrition estimates.
- Delete individual food entries.
- Export records as JSON backups.
- Import supported JSON backups.
- Clear saved application records.

Clearing application records does not necessarily remove copies retained in Anna's platform logs or backups. Requests involving platform-managed data may need to be directed to Anna.

## 7. Data Retention

Application records remain in the active storage until they are replaced or cleared by the user, subject to platform storage behavior.

Anna may apply separate retention periods to operational data, service requests, and backups.

## 8. International Processing

Because the application runs on Anna, information submitted to its services may be processed outside your country or region, depending on Anna and its service providers.

## 9. Health Disclaimer

Qingheng AI provides approximate nutritional and weight management information for general wellness purposes.

It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment, or professional dietary advice.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Qingheng AI is intended for adults aged 18 or older, consistent with Anna's platform requirements.

## 11. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when application features, data processing practices, or applicable requirements change. The effective date will be updated accordingly.
